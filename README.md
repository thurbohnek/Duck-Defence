# Quackpocalypse

© 2026 Ørjan Grane / Studio.grane. All rights reserved.

A rubber-duck arena survivor roguelite: the grime apocalypse has arrived, and one brave rubber duck stands in its way. Swim around, let your weapons fire automatically, pop waves of grime, collect pearls, level up and evolve your weapons. Survive 12 minutes and defeat the Loofah King.

## Play

Play it at **https://thurbohnek.github.io/Quackpocalypse/** or open `index.html` in any modern browser. Everything (graphics, sound, music) is in this single file — no build step, no dependencies.

## Controls

- **Touch:** choose in Settings between Floating stick, Fixed stick, Arrow buttons, Tap to swim, Swipe and Tilt (fixed stick and arrows can be placed left, middle or right in three sizes)
- **Keyboard:** WASD or arrow keys to move, Esc/P to pause, 1/2/3 to pick level-up cards, R to reroll

## Features

- 10 stages, each with its own hazard, new enemies and final boss: The Bathtub, Kitchen Sink, Swimming Pool, Laundry Room, Garden Pond, Hot Spring, Tide Pool, Frozen Lake, The Sewer and the Grime Factory finale

- 16 weapons, each with 5 levels and an evolution (8 unlocked by clearing stages, see the Armory)
- 13 passive items
- 40 bosses (4 per stage) and dozens of enemy types
- 9 unlockable ducks with their own starting weapon and traits
- Permanent upgrades bought with rubber chips
- Water Temperature difficulty levels after your first win
